# Night City v6 / art direction

The v6 interface drew visual and motion inspiration from the [Cyberpunk 2077 site](https://www.cyberpunk.net/us/en/), [Project E3: UI](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=60MpEkIcm3g), and [Cyberpunk 2077 - Concept UI](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ilYgQdyBFf4). Its yellow and black hero, clipped navigation, framed project modules, and restrained motion were implemented with original code; no reference code, logos, or character art were reused.

`assets/night-city-engineer-v6.png` is AI-generated fictional key art. It is not a portrait or a photograph of a build. This historical asset is not the active background; see [PHOTO-CREDITS.md](PHOTO-CREDITS.md) for the current photograph and [ART-DIRECTION-V8.md](ART-DIRECTION-V8.md) for the later generated artwork.
